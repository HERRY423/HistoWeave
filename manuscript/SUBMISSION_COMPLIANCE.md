## Critical submission blockers

1. **LLM-policy compliance.** This P1 working draft received OpenAI Codex assistance. It must not be uploaded as though it were a compliant human-authored final manuscript. Human authors must substantively rewrite and verify it and document only permitted assistance, or obtain written guidance from the Editorial Office.
2. **Author metadata.** Full names, order, affiliations, postal addresses, corresponding email, ORCID, CRediT roles, funding, acknowledgements, and conflicts are unknown and cannot be inferred.
3. **Independent non-oracle validation.** The current Wu stress test is one study, lacks an independent public timestamp, and uses oracle \(K\). It cannot support non-oracle personalisation.
4. **LOOCV editorial risk.** The five-dataset analysis is scientifically useful as a negative diagnostic, but the journal states that leave-one-out ML papers are normally rejected without special justification.
5. **Incomplete aligned SOTA coverage.** SpaGCN, STAGATE, GraphST, and BayesSpace are absent from the external five-dataset panel; only BANKSY overlaps.

## Claim boundary retained

The present evidence supports an implemented, fail-closed evidence-governance protocol and a global-default decision under the evaluated evidence. It does **not** support:

- superior personalised method selection on unseen studies;
- non-oracle deployment validation;
- a complete aligned SOTA comparison across platforms;
- transport of spatial-region results to cell-type labels; or
- a reliable ISUS predictor of spatial-method gain.
