# Author-supplied metadata required before submission

Do not infer or fabricate these fields.

- Final title approval
- Full author names and order
- Department, institution, city, postal code, country for each affiliation
- Corresponding-author name, email, telephone if requested, and validated ORCID
- CRediT roles for every author
- Funding agencies and exact grant numbers
- Acknowledgements
- Competing-interest declaration for every author
- Related preprints, dissertations, reports, or manuscripts
- Confirmation that all authors approved the final version
- Accurate final AI/LLM disclosure after resolving the journal-policy blocker
- Suggested and opposed reviewers, with conflict checks
